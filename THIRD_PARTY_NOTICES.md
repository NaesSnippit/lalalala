## PMD reference

The visual reference is https://sizztuna.neocities.org/pmdtests/ultimate . Its PKMN Mystery Dungeon font identifies a FontStruct Non-Commercial License. FontStruct states that its FontStruct licenses prohibit redistribution without the designer's permission: https://fontstruct.com/news/2013/07/ . That font is therefore not included in this release. VT323 supplies the pixel typography instead. Earlier music, sound effects, and background images are also omitted from this simplified version.
